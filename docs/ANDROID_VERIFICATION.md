Its Chrome 83 WebView keeps the app inspectable over authorized ADB despite the release flag and explicit `setWebContentsDebuggingEnabled(false)`. An initial socket check ran before the engine finished starting; the delayed check and real inspection endpoint corrected that result. No device restrictions or firmware settings were changed.
